What e-Claim is

e-Claim is where staff expenses and supplier bills are captured, checked, approved, and paid — and where the carbon-relevant lines are handed to CarbonNext, which does the emissions maths. e-Claim itself does no carbon calculation; it captures clean data and forwards it.

You drop a receipt (photo or PDF) or add a mileage trip. The system reads it, you confirm it, an approver signs it off, and on release it goes two ways at once: all lines export to your accounting/ERP for reimbursement, and the carbon-relevant lines post to CarbonNext. Everything is tenant-isolated per company and leaves a tamper-evident audit trail.

TIP Two document types, one door

A receipt is something a person paid and claims back (a staff expense). A vendor bill is an invoice the company pays a supplier. You can drop either here — receipts become claims; if one is really a vendor bill, you switch it on the next screen.

The spine

The five-step flow

Every claim moves through the same five stages. The green stepper at the top of a claim shows where it is.

1

Capture

Drop receipts or add mileage. The system reads vendor, amount, date.

2

Verify

The uploader checks each line and confirms nothing was misread.

3

Approve

An approver signs off — gated by the approval matrix & their limit.

4

Release

Carbon lines post to CarbonNext; all lines export to the ERP.

5

Pay

Finance marks the reimbursement paid — a different person than the maker.

People

Who's who

Four roles decide what each person can see and do. Two see the whole firm; two see only the companies granted to them.

Partner

Full access

Everything, including all Manage screens. Can approve any amount and is the only role that satisfies partner-only approval bands.

Manager

Runs the firm

Same admin access as a partner; approves within their personal limit and the matrix band (e.g. up to RM 2,000).

Approver

Capture & approve

Works on the companies granted to them: captures claims, and approves within the matrix. Cannot open the Manage admin screens.

Viewer

Read-only

Can look but not touch — no capturing, approving, or paying. Useful for auditors and finance observers.

HEADS-UP An approver can't approve everything

Under the approval matrix, a claim above a band's ceiling needs a more senior role. So an Approver may legitimately be blocked from approving a large claim — that's the control working, not a bug.

Capturing · sidebar: + New claim

New claim

The "Submit an expense claim" screen. Each receipt or trip you add becomes one line on a single claim.

Claim detailseveryone who captures

Who the claim is for, and how it's grouped.

  • Employee — pick the person the claim belongs to (from the Employees register). Their reference and department travel to CarbonNext.
  • Claim type / Event / Purpose — optional grouping and business justification.
Receiptsreads automatically

Drop images or PDFs — each becomes one line, read by OCR.

Supported: JPG · PNG · WebP · PDF, or a ZIP of many. The vendor, amount, date and expense type are read for you; a hard-to-read photo is flagged VERIFY MANUALLY so you fill it in yourself on the next screen. Every receipt becomes an expense-claim line — nothing is silently moved elsewhere.

Mileage tripsreads distance

From / To (and stops) — the server computes the real driving distance.

Pick each address from the suggestions, choose the vehicle, and the km × rate is priced for you. A longer-than-shortest route is flagged to the approver. Reimbursed at the company mileage rate.

The declaration & Submitrequired

Tick the out-of-pocket declaration, then Submit.

Because these are expenses you're claiming back, you confirm you paid them yourself and won't be reimbursed elsewhere. Submit is disabled until a receipt finishes reading and the declaration is ticked.

Capturing · the review screen

Verify & review

One screen carries a claim from capture to release. What you can do depends on the claim's status and your role.

Verify the contentthe uploader

Open each line, correct anything the OCR misread, then confirm.

If Submitter verification is on, an uploaded claim waits for you to press Confirm content & send to review — only a verified claim reaches the approver. If a line is really a vendor bill, use the switch to move it out of the claim.

Decide the claimthe approver

Approve, query, or reject — per line, or the whole claim.

  • Decide claim — set each line to Approve / Query / Reject (a query or reject needs a reason).
  • Approve all / Send back / Reject all — whole-claim shortcuts. Reject is final and asks for confirmation.
The decision footeralways visible

A sticky bar shows the claim's identity and live totals with the action on the right.

It stays on screen no matter how long the lines or audit trail are, so Release (or Confirm, Resubmit) is always one click away. The green leaf on a line means it's carbon-related and will post to CarbonNext on release.

Inbox · claim queues

The claim queues

The Inbox group in the sidebar is the same claim list filtered by status, with a live count badge on each. This is where you find work.

All claimseveryone

Every claim you can see, searchable and exportable.

Search by title, vendor or reference. Export CSV downloads the list for accounting. A status dropdown filters to any single state.

To verify (uploads)uploaders

submitted Claims parked for the uploader to confirm.

Appears only when Submitter verification is on. Each claim here is waiting for the person who uploaded it to check the content and press Confirm — only then does it reach an approver.

Awaiting reviewapprovers

in review Claims waiting for an approver to sign off.

The approver's main queue. Open a claim, verify the lines, and decide.

Needs attentioneveryone

Claims flagged for a second look.

Where possible duplicates and items needing a fix surface, so nothing quietly stalls.

To releaseapprovers

approved Approved claims waiting to be released.

Releasing is the step that posts carbon lines to CarbonNext and exports all lines to the ERP. A claim needing accounting coding is coded here first.

Rejectedeveryone

rejected Claims turned down — final.

A rejected claim is terminal and carries the reason the approver gave. Start a fresh claim if it needs to be resubmitted.

Releasedeveryone

Carbon lines have posted, all lines have exported, and the claim is locked. Corrections happen by reversal, never by editing released data.

Do the work

Approvals & Payables

Approvalsapprovers

The approver's focused inbox of claims to decide.

Everything sitting in review that you're allowed to act on. Each row links straight to the claim's review screen. The separation-of-duties rule blocks you from approving a claim you created yourself.

Payablesfinance

Released reimbursements and approved vendor bills to mark paid.

  • Two tabs: Staff reimbursements and Vendor bills, each with an honest per-currency total (never a mixed RM sum).
  • Mark paid records payment — and the person paying can't be the one who created the claim.
  • An approved-but-unreleased claim shows Release to pay instead — release first, then pay.

Vendor bills side

Vendor bills & AP invoices

The supplier-invoice half of the system — bills the company pays, kept separate from staff reimbursements.

Vendor billspartner / manager

A holding queue for documents read as vendor invoices.

When a company runs in divert mode, uploads the classifier reads as supplier invoices land here. From a bill you can File as AP invoice, or bring it back into a claim if it's really a staff expense. In the default keep mode, nothing lands here from the claim form.

AP invoicesfinance

Code, approve and pay supplier invoices.

The accounts-payable flow: assign the accounting coding, route through the AP approval matrix, then pay — with the same separation-of-duties (the coder, filer and approver must differ). Export the ones still to post as a CSV.

Carbon

CarbonNext handoff & coverage

e-Claim forwards raw data; CarbonNext owns the scope, factors and tCO₂e. These two screens show what was sent and how completely.

CarbonNext handoffeveryone

The log of carbon-relevant lines forwarded to CarbonNext.

Every released carbon line becomes one record here — category, amount, quantity & unit, vendor, date, employee, vehicle type. A correction shows as a reversal (a negative record), never an edit. This is raw data, not carbon maths.

Carbon coverageeveryone

Captured vs forwarded, per period — the honest gap.

Shows the full document spend captured against the carbon lines forwarded. Because only carbon-relevant lines forward, a bill with non-carbon lines forwards less than its total — that gap is shown on purpose and reconciles by document reference.

Setup & manage · partner / manager only

Setup & manage

The Manage group configures how a company works. These screens are open to partners and managers only.

Employeespartner / manager

The register of people who submit claims.

Name, employee reference, cost centre, position, department. You pick an employee when capturing, and their details forward to CarbonNext. These people don't log in — they're who a claim is for.

Userspartner / manager

The login accounts and what they can do.

  • Set each person's role, authority limit (their personal approval cap), and which companies they may work on.
  • Deactivate to block sign-in immediately. You can't demote or deactivate your own account, and you can't create someone more senior than yourself.
Categoriespartner / manager

The expense categories staff pick from.

Each category carries one carbon flag: carbon-related lines forward to CarbonNext; others go to the ERP only. This single flag is all e-Claim decides — CarbonNext does the emissions maths.

Approval matrixpartner / manager

Who may approve which amount band.

Set bands like ≤ RM 2,000 → a manager, above → a partner. Apply a starter template or add bands by hand. An amount above the top band fails closed (someone must configure it), and every change is written to the audit trail.

Exchange ratespartner / manager

Monthly FX rates for foreign-currency receipts.

A foreign receipt converts to a MYR value using the month's rate. A claim with an unconverted foreign line can't be approved until its rate is set — so the RM total (and the approval gate) is never incomplete.

Vehiclespartner / manager

The vehicle registry for mileage claims.

Register vehicles with a type (the type is what CarbonNext computes emissions from). Any vehicle is selectable on a mileage trip — handy when someone claims on another person's behalf.

Events & budgetspartner / manager

Group claims under a trip or activity with a budget.

Attach many people's claims to one event (a trip, a training) and track spent-vs-budget. An event's cost centre flows down onto its claims' lines.

Settingspartner / manager

Per-company behaviour toggles.

  • Submitter verification — uploads park until the uploader confirms them.
  • Capture: vendor-bill routing — keep (every receipt becomes a claim) vs divert (auto-sort vendor bills).
  • FX auto-prefill — pull the month's rate automatically, or key every rate by hand.
  • Carbon reversals — how corrections to released carbon data are handled.

Reference

The safety controls

A few rules run underneath every screen. They exist to keep money and carbon data trustworthy — if one blocks you, it's usually doing its job.

A Out-of-pocket declaration

Before a claim with expenses is submitted, the claimant confirms they paid it themselves and won't double-claim. Required at capture; checked again at release.

B Separation of duties

The person who creates a claim can't approve it, and the person who pays it can't be the one who created it. Enforced in the database and the app.

C Submitter verification

When on, an OCR-built claim waits for its uploader to confirm the content is right before it reaches an approver — only a verified transaction goes forward.

D Vendor-bill routing

Default keep: every receipt on the claim form becomes a claim line — switch it on review if it's really a bill. divert restores auto-sorting to Vendor Bills.

E Append-only ledger

Released carbon records, release batches and the audit trail can't be edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected by a reversal, so the history always stays honest.

! If something's disabled

A greyed Submit usually means a receipt is still reading or the declaration isn't ticked. A blocked Approve usually means the matrix needs a more senior role, or a foreign line needs its exchange rate.
